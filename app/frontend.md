# Philosophy Newsletter: Trouillot's Crystal Ball 

A newsletter that blends the day's most pressing current affairs with interesting takes from historians and political philosophers. 

## Your First Task 
Redesign the front page of this website. It should fit the brand aesthetics: Erudite, subtly old-fashioned with good taste. I would imagine the background to be parchment-paper coloured, for instance. There should be a black banner on top: Top-left is logo, top-right has clickable white words to other pages. 

[ ] Design a custom logo, titled Trouillot's Crystal Ball. "Trouillot" should be on top, and "Crystal Ball" below.
[ ] The text should be at the center. Header 1: "Drawing from the Past"; Subtitle 3: "Incorporating the week's news with perspectives from history and political philosophy." Below it, the subscribe bar. 

## Your Second Task 
[ ] On the home page, one of the white words should be "Article Repository". This should link to a sub-page where all articles are written there. Make sure this sub-page is similar but not identical to the home page in design style. 
[ ] In the backend, modify the code such that when the output is ready (the final article), a horizontal thumbnail is created on that page with the article title and date. When the thumbnail is clicked, the viewer should be able to read the whole article.
